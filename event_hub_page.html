Upcoming Events

Christmas

This is the best holiday of the year! It is during winter where you can make snow angles and snow men and drink hot cocoa and make gingerbread houses. Santa will come and bring you gifts. Happens on December 25 every year.

Thanksgiving

This is where you can spend time with your family preparing a big, delicious feast on November 26 every year. People usually prepare turkey stuffed with a variety of ingredients.

Halloween

This is a holiday wear kids (and adults) dress up in costumes and knock on neighbors' doors for tricks or treats and candy.

Summer Break

This is a break during the summer where kids have no school. People can go to the beach and have fun. This break is usually around 3 months long starting in June and ending in August but can vary depending on what school you go to and where you are located.
