Rise Beyond

This movie is about a young boy named Ethan Carter who grew up in the city. He wants to become a famous, world-wide selling singer. He stands on stage with a single guitar, ready to shock the world with his talent. He must rise beyond all the hate and malicious words to reach his true potential.
