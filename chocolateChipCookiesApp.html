Chocolate Chip Cookies

This recipe makes chocolate chip cookies. Chocolate chip cookies are brown sugar cookies with chocolate chips in them. They are a classic cookie and many people love this all around the world.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Chocolate Chips
  • Egg
  • Butter
  • Baking Soda
  • White Sugar
  • Brown Sugar
  • Salt
  • Vanilla Extract

Instructions

  1. Mix the melted butter, white sugar, brown sugar, salt, vanilla extract, baking soda, and egg in one bowl
  2. Now add the flour and mix, then add the chocolate chips and mix it all up
  3. Next, roll into balls on parchment paper/li>
  4. Lastly, heat the oven to 350 degrees fahrenheit and bake the cookies
